1. INTRO

Hello! My name is Viyro, my background comes from countless hours of ARPG's, Stream Management, and Event Creation. You can find some of my past work in POE via Zizarans Youtube Channel, defining meta builds across many games etc, as well as the Co-Creation of Path of Exiles Gauntlet. Now that thats out the way lets get in to the build.

The purpose of this build is to min-max our ability to do all of the games content including Labyrinth. My latest Diablo Lab kill was under 10 seconds.

My philosophy on Diablo 2 build creation is mostly problem solving first which should hopefully give you insight on gear choices.

To “Solve” a build we must solve all issues that plague a character in Diablo 2. Lab is the ultimate check in to this for your character. In no particular order. We can use a combination of the character and Mercenary to achieve these things. 

Required:

  • Physical immunity breaking
  • Leech
  • 95% Max Res
  • Block Chance
  • Overcap res to accommodate for Lower Resist and Conviction
  • Physical Damage Reduction to accommodate for Amp / Decrep etc
  • Hit Chance
  • Defense Rating
  • - Enemy Physical Resistance
  • “Crit” - Deadly Strike, Critical Strike Chance
  • Damage
  • Open Wounds Source to stop regeneration on bosses
  • Auras
  • Cannot be Frozen

2. MAIN STATS

Pretty simple here. Strength to equip all gear (including on swap), Dexterity to Block Cap, Vitality rest of points.

4. GEARING PROGRESSION

With the above list for solve, please keep that in mind as you swap gear around. I would not personally play Carnage till I had a minimum level of gear I was happy with, to instantly go in to Lab. I will start with best in slot on the left, going down the list.

  • Weapon: The Grandfather, Harrogath's Last Stand, Grandmaster's Glory. Cheap Beginner Item: Broken Promise Runeword, or even cheaper, Onyx's Solar Flair & Onyx's Super Nova 
    Helmet: Insanity, Prowess in Battle. Note: This is our solve for fanaticism aura which will cut the amount of Attack Speed we need elsewhere. You can also solve this with Ragnarok on your Merc for example and use a different helmet. Use the IAS Calculator properly HERE. 
  • Armor: Solace (This is our Cleansing Aura Solve), Qual-Kehk's Oath
  • Shield: Braced for Battle (This is our Concentration Aura Solve, you can use Pride on Merc), Stormshield (PDR %)
  • Gloves: Steelrend (Open Wounds Solve), Black Lotus
  • Amulet: Sigil of Hope, Highlord's Wrath, The Vanquisher, Metalgrid
  • Rings: Contricting Ring (You may opt to use 2 of these till you get enough max res elsewhere), Jackal's Laughter, Bul-Kathos' Wedding Band, Raven Frost (If you need Cannot be Frozen)
  • Belt: Kashya's Ward, Ogre's Embrace, The Art of War
  • Boots: Zero Effect, Stallion Hooves(Bis in maps), 

Weapon Swap:

  • Bis: 2x +4 Nightscape filled with Heaven Facet's
  • Other Option: 1x Heart of the Oak + 1x Call to Arms

Buffing Items:

  • Fade: Cloaker Beast
  • Holy Shield: Savant Sin or Knight's Dawn
  • Enchant: Demon Limb / Todesfaelle Flamme
