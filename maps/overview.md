# Maps: Getting Started

Maps are consumable items that open endgame areas in **Hell difficulty**. Fight through a themed area, discover optional encounters, and challenge its **Warden** for loot, another map, and mapping currency.

There are **10 themes**, each with **6 tiers**. Higher tiers bring tougher monsters, denser packs, and more modifiers. The modifiers on an individual map can make a big difference to your build, so always read its tooltip before opening it.

## Where do I get my first map?

Farm monsters in these areas on **Hell difficulty** for a chance at a **Tier 1 map**:

- The Ancients' Way
- Icy Cellar
- Worldstone Keep Level 1
- Worldstone Keep Level 2
- Worldstone Keep Level 3
- Throne of Destruction

Maps are a chance drop; you will not get one from every run. Clear the monsters in these areas rather than relying on a Baal kill. The Forsaken Labyrinth is separate content and is not a source of these starter-map drops.

Once you start mapping, map monsters can drop more maps and mapping currency. Defeating the Warden is especially valuable: its reward includes **one map and one mapping currency**, alongside its regular loot rolls.

## Opening your first map

1. Launch D2R Reimagined through **D2RLoader** with the **Maps plugin enabled**.
2. Enter a game on **Hell difficulty** and go to **Harrogath**, the Act V town.
3. Read your map's tooltip. Check the tier and any modifiers that could be dangerous for your build.
4. Put **one map by itself** in the Horadric Cube and press **Transmute**.
5. Enter the red portal, explore the map, and find the entrance to the Warden's arena.

Opening a map consumes the item. You do not need an orb, shard, relic, or scroll to open it; those currencies are used to craft maps before a run.

## What should I expect inside?

Each map has a main exploration area and a separate Warden arena. Expect familiar monster types in larger, more dangerous groups, with different populations and boss abilities for each theme. Ordinary monsters can retain their usual immunities, so plan how your build will handle them.

The map tooltip and in-map display show the rolled modifiers and combat Magic Find bonus. Some rolls increase enemy numbers or add monster auras; others reduce your resistances, attack damage, or skills. Optional events and rare treasure monsters give you a reason to explore beyond the direct route to the Warden.

**Start with Tier 1.** Being able to clear late Hell is a useful starting point, but a difficult modifier can still make a low-tier map dangerous. Upgrade when your current tier feels comfortable, and reroll maps that are a poor match for your build.

## Tiers at a glance

Area level describes the area, **not a required character level**.

| Map tier | Area level | Rolled modifiers | How to obtain it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Tier 1 | 100 | 2 | Late-Hell starter drops and map rewards |
| Tier 2 | 101 | 2–3 | Upgrade Tier 1, or find it while mapping |
| Tier 3 | 102 | 3-4 | Upgrade Tier 2, or find it while mapping |
| Tier 4 | 103 | 4 | Upgrade Tier 3, or find it while mapping |
| Tier 5 | 104 | 4-5 | Upgrade Tier 4, or find it while mapping |
| Tier 6 | 105 | 6 | Corrupt Tier 5 with a Worldstone Shard |

Tier 6 is a substantial difficulty jump. It cannot drop directly, and a Horadric Orb cannot make one. See [crafting and progression](/en/maps/crafting) before spending your first Worldstone Shard.

## Starting another run

**Run each combination of theme and tier only once per game.** To run another map of that same theme and tier, start a new game.

For example, after running a Tier 1 Sandswept Tomb, you can run a Tier 2 Sandswept Tomb or a Tier 1 Frozen Depths in the same game. Another Tier 1 Sandswept Tomb needs a new game, even if the second item has different modifiers.

Returning to town and re-entering your existing map continues the same run. It does not refresh enemies, reroll modifiers, or reset event rewards. Finish the run and collect your loot before leaving the game.

## If a map will not open

Check that you are in **Hell**, standing in **Harrogath**, and transmuting **exactly one map with nothing else in the Cube**. If you have already visited that theme and tier, start a new game. Also check that the Maps plugin is enabled and that your mod and plugin are up to date.

For changing a map before opening it, use the recipes on [Crafting and Progression](/en/maps/crafting).
