## Gameplay Changes

### Charm Weight System

**Weight Limits:**
- Small Charms: weight 1
- Large Charms: weight 2
- Grand Charms: weight 3

**⚠️ At charm weight 41 or higher: maximum mana is set to zero**

Check your advanced stats panel to view your charm weight.

![charmies.png](/charmies.png)

### Difficulty

#### **Normal**
- Easy introduction to learn class changes
- Bosses and side quests may have additional enemies if you venture early

#### **Nightmare**
- Difficulty ramps up around mid Act 3
- By Act 5 end: area levels reach 70-75
- **Nightmare Terror Zone level cap: 80**
- **Drops up to CHAM rune and very rarely a ZOD**
- **Sunder Charms available from Terror Zones**

#### **Hell**
- Scaled for levels 90+ with Terror Zone level cap 99
- **Recommended to farm Nightmare Terror Zones until level 85+**
- Ensure resistances are capped and build is refined before attempting
- **Monster HP: ~4x higher | Damage: ~1.5x higher** (all player counts)

#### **Terror Zones**
- Encompass entire acts and rotate every hour
- Special locations in Act 4: Cows, Stony Field, Tristram (previously unavailable there)
- **Cow portal:** Use Wirt's Leg in Cube (unlimited uses; originally recipe still works)
- Cow Waypoint unlocks after first secret level visit

## **Quality of Life**

### Socket Punching

There are two ways to add sockets to items:

#### Jewel Punching
**Cube an item + Jewel (same rarity)**
- **Magic/White items** use Magic Jewels
- **Rare/Set items** use Rare Jewels
- **Crafted/Unique items** use Unique Jewels
- Add specific number of sockets (respects item limits)
- [Socket Recipes](https://wiki.d2r-reimagined.com/en/recipes/CubeRecipes#socket-recipes)

#### Orb of Socketing
- Randomly rolls **1-6 sockets**
- Works on Set and Unique items too!
- Respects maximum socket limit
- [Orb of Socketing](https://wiki.d2r-reimagined.com/en/Items/Orbs#orb-of-socketing)

**Socket Limits:** Shown as `(item level):sockets`
- Example: `(1+):6` = up to 6 sockets at item level 1+
- Check [Base Items](https://www.d2r-reimagined.com/bases) page for per-item limits



### Rune Stacks

#### Convert Runes ↔ Stacks
- **Single → Stack:** Cube a single rune by itself
- **Stack → Single:** Cube a rune stack to get a useable rune

#### Upgrade Runes (Stack Rune Up)
**Cube 2 single runes (or stack with qty 2+) + TP Tome**
- Creates 1 rune of next tier higher
- Reduces stack quantity by 2
- Tome returned full!

![](/210imageassets/stackuprecipe210.png)

![](/210imageassets/stackupresult210.png)

#### Downgrade Runes (Stack Rune Down)
**Cube 1 rune (or rune stack) + ID Tome**
- Creates 1 rune of next tier lower
- Reduces stack quantity by 1
- Tome returned full!

![](/210imageassets/stackdownrecipe210.png)

![](/210imageassets/stackdownresult210.png)

## Suggested Farming Areas

> **Tip:** Terror Zones offer better loot than boss farming. Farm in **Nightmare Terror Zones** until ready for Hell.

📋 See [Loot Table page](https://wiki.d2r-reimagined.com/en/Items/LootTable) for TZ-exclusive loot

### Act 1
- Countess Tower
- Tristram
- Barracks
- Catacombs (up to Andariel for added monster packs)

### Act 2
- Arcane Sanctuary
- Canyon of the Magi
- Tal Rasha Tombs

### Act 3
- Flayer Jungle
- Great Marsh (if pygmy spawn)
- Travincal
- Durance of Hate (through Mephisto for council monsters)

### Act 4
- **River of Flame** (Hephasto + monster groups if you can defeat Kudaii; otherwise skip)
- **Chaos Sanctuary**
- **Cow Level** (use Wirt's Leg in Cube; available after Baal defeat)

### Act 5
- **Rectifier & Shenk** (near Frigid Highlands waypoint, multiple uniques)
- **Halls of Pain & Halls of Vaught**
- **Worldstone Keep & Throne of Destruction** (high density; Baal quick to clear)
