Classes

Amazon

Magic Arrow, Cold Arrow, and Fire Arrow

  • Now fire one arrow at base level, then gain an additional arrow at level 3 and every 3 soft points after, up to 9 arrows.
  • The three central arrows can leech and trigger effects.

 

Exploding Arrow and Freezing Arrow

  • Now fire one arrow at base level, then gain an additional arrow at 10 and 20 soft points, up to 3 arrows.
  • Only the central arrow can leech and trigger effects.

 

Druid

Werewolf

  • Now gains 10-70% velocity as it levels.

Werewolf and Werebear Charge Skills

  • Feral Rage and Maul now start with 2 maximum charges, gain one charge at level 4 and every 3 hard points thereafter, and cap at 10 charges.

 

Maul

  • Increased attack-rating scaling to 50% base and 15% per level.
  • Charges now grant 1% Crushing Blow each, plus scaling attack speed and enhanced damage per charge.

 

Feral Rage

  • Removed the movement-speed bonus and direct base damage.
  • Each charge now grants 2% Life Steal and 2% enemy Physical Resistance reduction.
  • Added charge-based Enhanced Damage and -Enemy Defense per Hit, both scaling with skill level.

 

Rabies

  • Reduced attack-rating scaling to 20% base and 10% per level.
  • Mana cost now starts at 5 and increases by 1 per level.

 

Toxic Fangs

  • Now applies the same -20% enemy Poison Resistance effect as Rabies.
  • Reduced poison duration to 0.4 seconds and substantially increased poison damage.
  • Increased synergy scaling from 10% to 13%, increased attack-rating scaling, and added a physical-damage curve.
  • Increased the starting mana cost to 2.

 

General

  • Updated several Druid tooltips for clearer, more consistent stat and mana-cost presentation.
