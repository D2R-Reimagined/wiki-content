# 3.0.12
 <br> <br>
## D2RLoader Support
* Adds in support for stash search extension
- Removed obsolete mainmenupanelHD.json causing positioning problems for D2RLoader, verified no issues non-loader launch visually
 <br>
## Class
 <br> <br>
### Druid
- Adjusted Mark of the Wolf to give 20% Deadly Strike and 4% Avoidance instead of AR and Life
- Updated many unique pelts stats and names
  - https://github.com/D2R-Reimagined/d2r-reimagined-mod/pull/1323
  
 <br> <br>
### Sorceress
- Frozen Orb
  - Reduced explosion radius of individual bolts from 5 to 3
 <br>

>[!Note]
> These bolts were way overperforming in big groups, if this doesn't tone it down enough then we will start adjusting the curves down.
 
 <br> <br> 
### Necromancer
- Raise Skeleton
  - Adjusted health to sane values, updated tooltips formula to reflect the true health the summons have.
- Raise Skeletal Mage
  - Adjusted health to sane values, updated tooltips formula to reflect the true health the summons have.

>[!Note]
>This little rabbit hole + some advice from Ben  and a post from celestialray has shown we have a severe over-stat problem with all minions attack rating and defense we will address in a later pull request and patch.

 <br> <br>

## Monsters
- All Uber bosses now have mephisto monprop (30ias/30fcr/sunder all)
* **Bishibosh**
  * Removed **Fire immunity** in **Normal and Nightmare**.
  * Replaced **Fire Enchanted** with **Multishot**.
* **Boneash**
  * Removed **Cold immunity** in **Normal, Nightmare, and Hell**.
  * Replaced **Magic Resistant** with **Lightning Enchanted**.
* **Coldworm the Burrower**
  * Removed **Cold immunity** in **Normal, Nightmare, and Hell**.
  * Replaced **Magic Resistant** with **Lightning Enchanted**.
* **Fire Eye**
  * Removed **Fire immunity** in **Hell**.
  * Replaced **Fire Enchanted** with **Strong**.
* **Witch Doctor Endugu**
  * Removed **Fire immunity** in **Normal and Nightmare**.
  * Replaced **Fire Enchanted** with **Mana Burn**.
* **The Tormentor** (not currently in use)
  * Removed **Fire immunity** in **Normal, Nightmare, and Hell**.
  * Removed **Lightning immunity** in **Normal, Nightmare, and Hell**.
  * Replaced **Magic Resistant + Fire Enchanted** with **Mana Burn + Cold Enchanted**.
* **The Cow King**
  * Removed **Lightning immunity** in **Normal**.
  * Replaced **Magic Resistant** with **Strong**.
  * Added **Fast** as a third modifier.
  * Remains Lightning immune in **Nightmare and Hell**.
* **Axe Dweller** (not currently in use)
  * Removed **Fire immunity** in **Normal, Nightmare, and Hell**.
  * Replaced **Magic Resistant + Fire Enchanted** with **Strong + Fast + Curse**.
* **Betsy, The Cow Queen**
  * Removed **Lightning immunity** in **Normal**.
  * Replaced **Magic Resistant** with **Mana Burn**.
  * Remains Lightning immune in **Nightmare and Hell**.

## Items
- Warlock +skill tab prefixes now have a light purple color transform on grand charms instead of no color transform.
- Unique Colossal Jewels
  - Removed the CTC skills as all but 1 were broken or unwanted and the 1 desirable skill was the only jewel anyone wanted.
  - Added sunder to these jewels to match their corresponding elements as a source of sunder to free up a charm slot or give to your mercenary so they can do damage to immune enemies too.
  - Physical Damage jewel was reduced to 75-100% EWD and 20-40 Min/Max from 100-150 and 30-50
  - Increased the gold find %
>[!Note]
>Probably going to revisit these in the future when I have more time/motivation because I want to create a minion related one with an aura that only affects friendly monsters and buffs them in some manner, or gives an oSkill similar to the warlock death mark
