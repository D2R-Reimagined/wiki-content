Forsaken Labyrinth

A new Hell-difficulty endgame dungeon awaits beyond Harrogath. Explore a connected descent through five boss domains, with area levels rising from 100 to 110.

  • Face 50 distinct enemy types in the area's spawn pools, additional summoned reinforcements, seven wandering Forsaken Hero variants, and five main bosses: Maghda, Belial, Na-Krul, the Skeleton King, and Diablo.
  • Encounters introduce protective auras, dangerous spellcasters, summoned enemies, and a multi-stage Na-Krul fight. Wandering Forsaken Heroes use class-themed abilities and have class-equipment reward pools.
  • Obtain a Forsaken Token from Hell Baal's quest drop and transmute it in Harrogath to open the entrance. The token is returned by the recipe and can be reused.
  • Discover six new runes, endgame equipment, crafting materials, and the Relic of the Ancients. Meet Ord Rekar, Lorekeeper of the Ancients, at the entrance to learn about the Labyrinth and its rewards.

 

Relic of the Ancients

The Labyrinth introduces a new level-80 unique Grand Charm with +1 to a random class's skills, increased maximum life and mana, Magic Find, and Gold Find. Its soul display tracks your character's Labyrinth boss progress.

Paladin

Items and runewords

 

New runes and runewords

  • Added Brak, Zeph, Myr, Grit, Dem, and Hev, providing new physical, elemental, magic, attribute, and defensive socketing options. The rune stash supports all six on keyboard/mouse and controller.
  • Added six endgame runeword options: **Insanity, Chromaskin, Shatter, Ravage, Unity, Solace **. These offer combinations of auras, skills, resistance penetration, damage, and defenses.
  • Last Wish now accepts all weapon types.
  • Existing runewords receive a wider balance pass, including reduced Crushing Blow and slowing effects, revised defensive bonuses, and more physical resistance penetration. Despair now triggers Amplify Damage in place of Weaken.

 

Unique and set equipment

  • Crushing Blow is much less widely available. Many unique items, set bonuses, runewords, and affixes replace it with physical resistance penetration, Deadly Strike, Open Wounds, or other bonuses. Several remaining sources have lower values.
  • Many unique-item spell triggers change from on striking to on attack, with individual trigger chances and spell levels rebalanced. Slows Target is also reduced or replaced on numerous items.
  • A selection of existing uniques moves into the level-105 drop tier, including The Grandfather, Frost Wyrm, Lachdanan's Heart, Shadowtrick, Legacy of the Eternals, Haven of Light, Fortress of Morpheus, and El Druin. Several receive substantial reworks alongside this change.
  • Red Dragon Scales, Ghostly Chainmail, and Breastplate of Gond become endgame elemental armors with stronger offensive and defensive identities. Shambling Mound is upgraded as a poison-focused armor, while Tesla's Cuirass gains Arc triggers and lightning-focused bonuses.
  • Dreamscape becomes a Paladin shield built around Holy Shock. Scavanger's Carapace becomes a Paladin shield with Find Item, Magic Find, and casting support.
  • Night Hag and Vilifier receive major weapon reworks. Arachnid Mesh trades its slowing effect and Venom charges for revised mana and Energy bonuses.
  • Bigby's Crushing Fist is reworked into Hratli's Warden, replacing its scaling Crushing Blow with all resistances.
  • Jade Facet becomes a magic-damage jewel with magic skill damage and enemy magic resistance penetration. Tiger Eye is reworked around enhanced damage, Deadly Strike, and Open Wounds.
  • Existing sets receive targeted balance changes, with several bonuses trading Crushing Blow for physical penetration, Deadly Strike, Open Wounds, or other offensive effects.
  • Exceptional and elite gloves, boots, and belts have increased durability.
  • Heartbane and Nightcrawler become Necromancer shields supporting additional Skeleton Warriors and Skeletal Mages respectively.
  • Dire Wolf Might replaces its Crushing Blow bonus with a Might aura.

 

Socketing and affixes

  • Existing rune bonuses are rebalanced. Highlights include Dol granting Faster Cast Rate in weapons and flat mana in armor/shields, Amn granting Replenish Life in armor/shields, and Ber granting damage to demons instead of Crushing Blow in weapons.
  • Eld gains life after each kill in weapons and shields. Shael grants 15% weapon attack speed; Hel reduces requirements by 10%; Um grants 20% resistances in armor/shields.
  • Added elemental resistance penetration prefixes for weapons. Physical penetration replaces several Crushing Blow affixes on weapons, gloves, and charms.
  • Reduced elemental-absorption Coalescence rolls and several elemental skill-damage suffixes. Amethyst, Emerald, and Diamond weapon bonuses are also reduced.

 

Crafting and progression

  • Enchanting is now limited to one application per item. Enchant rewards and ingredient costs have been extensively rebalanced, with stronger options in many categories and new endgame choices using Labyrinth runes and Shadow Orbs.
  • Added enchanting options for arrows and bolts, including attack speed, Attack Rating, leech, Open Wounds, Deadly Strike, and item-finding bonuses.
  • Reworked high-end crafting around the new runes, with distinct physical, elemental, and magic weapon options. Several early and midgame crafts use cheaper or different runes, and magic boots can be crafted with an El rune for Faster Run/Walk.
  • Added classic-style Blood, Caster, Hit Power, and Safety crafting recipes, plus utility recipes for resistance jewelry and basic weapons. These use the mod's configured rewards; Blood Gloves do not grant Crushing Blow.
  • Removed the previous Worldstone Shard recipes for fixed-affix equipment and charms.
  • Added a Jade Facet recipe using a PreCrafted Black Cleft, a unique jewel, and 100 gems from a Gem Bag.
  • Hellfire Torch now needs five enhancement steps before its final conversion to Obsidian Beacon, down from ten. Each enhancement grants +2 attributes and +2 all resistances.
  • Obsidian Beacon now grants +3 all skills, +20 attributes, +20 all resistances, and 20% Faster Run/Walk, down from +4 skills and 25 in each of those other bonuses. Annihilus's intermediate upgrades no longer add experience gain, and Black Soulstone's experience bonus is reduced from 10% to 5%.
  • Corruption outcomes are rebalanced: several mana-regeneration rolls improve, the glove offensive result grants Deadly Strike and Open Wounds, and the weapon Crushing Blow result is reduced and no longer includes Deadly Strike.

 

Monsters, loot, and interface

  • Broadly reduced monster blocking, with most affected regular monsters standardized at 5%. Bosses and special enemies retain separate values.
  • Mercenaries gain 20% Dodge, Avoid, and Evade in Hell to boost their durability in the treacherous endgame content.
  • Increased monster density in the Blood Moor and Cold Plains, with a revised Normal-difficulty enemy mix in the Cold Plains.
  • Several enemy projectiles are faster or more threatening, including Diablo's lightning stream and Summoner's Glacial Spike. Selected spider poison durations are reduced.
  • Improved controller access to the Mod Information pages and their close controls.

D2RLoader ONLY Patch Notes

These will all be included in the upcoming Ladder

These patch notes only apply when using D2RLoader AND THE needed plugins to make this functionality work. You may grab the plugins from the ReimaginedLadder folder once it goes live

Unless a section says otherwise, a feature works on both Ladder launches and normal (non-ladder) D2RLoader launches. Settings marked Locked on Ladder are reset by the launcher on every ladder launch, so editing them has no effect there.

Keep in mind these are for both ladder and non-ladder. Some of the features may not work (like debug commands and config changes) in the ladder environment

Ladder Servers and Lobby

  • On a Ladder launch, the Multiplayer game browser lists games on the Reimagined ladder servers instead of player-hosted games
    • Host creates your game on a ladder server and joins you to it automatically
    • Game name up to 15 characters, password up to 15, description up to 31, 1-8 players, level difference 0-99
    • Difficulty, Expansion/RotW, Hardcore and Terrorized come from your character and the Host dialog
    • Servers are picked from the regions you selected in the launcher, closest first. If none has room, you're told to add another region in the launcher or try again in a moment
    • Joining a game from the list takes you to that game on its server. If a server briefly refuses the join, the game retries on its own up to 2 times
  • Ladder server ping readout
    • The status bar at the top of Multiplayer shows your best ladder region and its ping (e.g. na-east 32ms). The light is green under 120 ms, yellow under 200 ms and red above that
    • Hover the ? to see every region's ping, plus regions that didn't reply or have no open server
    • Ping is re-checked every 60 seconds while you're in the menus, never during a game
  • Changes on ladder launches
    • The main menu Play button is hidden. Ladder games start from Multiplayer
    • Games on your own PC (offline play, or double-clicking a character) are refused, because only the ladder servers save ladder characters
    • A new ladder character is created and saved, then you're sent back to the lobby with it. Its first automatic launch shows "failed to join", which is expected. Host or join from there
    • The lobby's server, account and redirect settings always use the launcher's values (Locked on Ladder)
  • Crash and freeze reports
    • On ladder launches, crashes and freezes (no new frame for 30 seconds) are sent to the Reimagined team with the lobby log. No account, IP address or save data is sent
    • To opt out, set sentry = false in d2rloader/config/lobby-bridge.toml

Non-ladder launches keep D2RLoader's normal lobby and hosting. If every region shows ???ms, a firewall may be blocking ping. Joining still works.

 

Server Saves

  • Ladder characters and the ladder shared stash are stored on the Reimagined servers
    • Each ladder has its own save folder, fully separate from your normal Reimagined characters. Ladder and non-ladder characters can never mix, and your normal characters are never touched
    • At launch, your ladder characters are downloaded from the server. The server's copy always wins: a local file that differs is replaced, and one the server doesn't know about is set aside
    • While you play, finished saves are uploaded in the background (checked about every 2 seconds). Half-written files are never sent
    • Minions saved by Bind And Summon travel with their character
    • A hardcore death removes the character from the server too
    • A new ladder save folder copies your graphics settings and loot filters from your normal profile
  • Backups and recovery

Loot Filter (UnHoarder by MindH1ve)
  • A JSON loot filter, bundled at version 1.2.9
    • Rules are ordered Show / Hide blocks. Matching stops at the first match unless a rule has Continue set
    • Rule actions: label text and background colour, custom item name, one of 16 drop sounds (Filter01-Filter16) and automap icons (circle, diamond, triangle or star, 12-40 px)
    • Conditions: item code, base name, item type, rarity (inferior, normal, superior, magic, rare, set, unique, crafted, tempered), item level (1-99), sockets, ethereal, identified, stack quantity and vendor sell price. Numbers accept equal / greater / less comparisons
  • The filter file is d2rloader/config/filter.json
    • Saving the file reloads it automatically. Ctrl+Shift+F9 reloads it by hand. An invalid file keeps the last working filter
  • The shipped starter filter hides nothing
    • Uniques and sets: gold diamond automap icon and drop sound Filter01
    • Maps and map currency: purple diamond automap icon
    • Runes, gems and Reimagined currencies: orange label text
  • Build your own filter with the loot filter builder on the Reimagined website
    • Presets: Starter (hides nothing), Semi Strict (hides inferior/normal, unsocketed, non-ethereal ordinary weapons and armor), Strict (also hides superior and magic ordinary weapons and armor)
    • Sound previews and download as filter.json. Signed-in accounts can save up to 50 filters

Hiding is visual only: hidden items aren't removed from the game. The filter can't see hidden information such as unidentified affixes or which unique an unidentified item is.

Character Trackers

Death Tracker
  • Counts your deaths per character and shows them in chat
    • /deaths: your total, e.g. "You have died 3 times. Last on 2026-10-24 14:05: Travincal (act 3).", plus deaths in the current game
    • /deaths log: your last 5 deaths with date, time and area
    • /help deaths: how to use the command
    • !deaths and !deaths log are a backup if the / command doesn't work. !commands lists the ! commands
  • A chat line each time a death is counted, e.g. "Death 4 - Chaos Sanctuary (act 4)." Turn it off with announce_deaths = false in d2rloader/config/death-tracker.toml
  • The lifetime total is never trimmed. The history keeps up to 200 deaths

Deaths are counted when you resurrect, so hardcore deaths aren't counted (see Hardcore Death Announcements). If you quit while dead and rejoin, that death isn't counted. Counts are stored on your PC in your Saved Games\Diablo II Resurrected folder, not on the website.

 

Kill Tracker
  • Counts the monsters you kill per character, lifetime total
    • /kills: e.g. "You have killed 12,480 monsters.", plus kills in the current game
    • /help kills: how to use the command
    • !kills is a backup if /kills doesn't work
    • Silent by default. To get a running total in chat every N kills, set announce_every = N in d2rloader/config/kill-tracker.toml
  • Saved every 30 seconds, and when you leave a game, type /kills or close the game

Kills made before the plugin was installed aren't counted. A crash can lose up to 30 seconds of kills. Counts are stored on your PC, not on the website.

 

Time Played
  • Tracks play time per character
    • /played: e.g. "Alice: 12h 34m 56s played. This game: 0h 8m 10s."
    • Time counts while your character is in a game, including town and AFK time. Menus and character select don't count
    • Saved every 30 seconds, on /played, when you leave a game and when you close the game

Tracking starts when the plugin is installed. Earlier play time can't be recovered. Stored on your PC, not on the website.
