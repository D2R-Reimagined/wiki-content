1. INTRO

Hello! My name is Viyro, my background comes from countless hours of ARPG's, Stream Management, and Event Creation. You can find some of my past work in POE via Zizarans Youtube Channel, defining meta builds across many games etc, as well as the Co-Creation of Path of Exiles Gauntlet. Now that thats out the way lets get in to the build.

The purpose of this build is to min-max our ability to do all of the games content including Labyrinth. My latest Diablo Lab kill was under 10 seconds.

My philosophy on Diablo 2 build creation is mostly problem solving first which should hopefully give you insight on gear choices.

To “Solve” a build we must solve all issues that plague a character in Diablo 2. Lab is the ultimate check in to this for your character. In no particular order. We can use a combination of the character and Mercenary to achieve these things. 

Required:

  • Physical immunity breaking
  • Leech
  • 95% Max Res
  • Block Chance
  • Overcap res to accommodate for Lower Resist and Conviction
  • Physical Damage Reduction to accommodate for Amp / Decrep etc
  • Hit Chance
  • Defense Rating
  • - Enemy Physical Resistance
  • “Crit” - Deadly Strike, Critical Strike Chance
  • Damage
  • Open Wounds Source to stop regeneration on bosses
  • Auras
  • Cannot be Frozen

Pretty simple here. Strength to equip all gear (including on swap), Dexterity to Block Cap, Vitality rest of points.

3. SKILLS

Your skills will be very reliant on where you are at in your solve with the gearing progression below.

Basic Mapping Setup and if in BIS for Labyrinth:
Max Carnage, Max Berserk, Max Melee Mastery, Max Battle Orders, 1 In to Battle Cry, Natural Resistance, Iron Skin, Shield Mastery, Increased Speed, Battle Command, and all Pre-requisites. Rest of points in to Bash.

Basic Labyrinth Setup when you first get there likely:
Max Carnage, Max Melee Mastery, Max Battle Cry, Max Battle Orders, 1 In to Natural Resistance, Iron Skin, Shield Mastery, Increased Speed, Battle Command, and all Pre-requisites. Rest of points in to Berserk.

6. MERCENARY

The goal of our mercenary is always going to be towards our solve above and keeping it alive. Your mercenary will likely change many times. Keep in mind you may opt for a different solve which means your Merc changes based on your needs. Maybe you value Holy Freeze from Act 2, Defiance, Might because you don't have Last Wish yet etc. The goal remains the same. Gear for Max Res, PDR, Leech etc to keep your merc alive. The Merc below facetanks most content. Prior to Solace you will likely want Cleansing on your Merc as well. There are a few solves for that.

BIS Merc - Act 1 Ice Bow Merc

  • Weapon: Last Wish (Might Aura)
  • Quiver: Rare with Amp / - Enemy Phys
  • Armor: Tarrasque Hide
  • Rings: Diablo's Soulstone Ring, Constricting Ring
  • Belt: Kashya's Ward
  • Boots: Zero Effect
  • Gloves: A lot of options here, I used Dracul's Grasp
  • Helmet: Andariel's Visage

Note: To get the Strength for Zero Effect, you must enchant Strength on a couple other items, I chose Andariel's Visage and Sigil of Hope.

Why: Inner Sight is insane and is our solve for Flat Defense Reduction making it easier for us to hit, this alone takes our hit chance from 55% to 90%+ on Diablo in Labyrinth. It also gives -enemy phys resistance which is very good.

7. DEGENERATE STUFF

Will start this section off by saying this is a section for people that already know what is going on, you may get to this point by the time you get this far! Do not do these steps unless you are aware of the rest of your gearing and what is changing.

Corruptions: Note you can double corrupt some items

  • Weapon: Crushing Blow (As our only Source), Physical Damage+Attack Speed
  • Armor: Make Armor Eth First, Max Res + All Res, Indestructible + 100% Enhanced Defense
  • Shield: Make Eth First, Indestructible + 100% Enhanced Defense,  Max Res + All Res
  • Gloves: Deadly Strike + Open Wounds, Enhanced Physical Damage + Armor Reduc
  • Amulet: Max Res + All Res, Enhcaned Physical Damage + Attack Speed
  • Rings: Max Res + All Res, PDR % + Magic Res
  • Belt: Max Res + All Res, PDR % + Magic Res
  • Boots: Make Eth First, Indestructible + 100% Enhanced Defense, Max Res + All Res, PDR % + Magic Res
  • Helm: Make Eth First, Indestructible + 100% Enhanced Defense, Max Res + All Res, PDR % + Magic Res

The more Max Res Corrupts you have the less you need in Runes, you can also drop Constricting Ring for another Jackal's Laughter which will change around sockets a lot.

Enchants:

  • Weapon: 100% Enchaned Weapon Damage, -10% To Enemy Physical Damage Resistance, +300 Attack Rating
  • Armor: Make Eth First, 100% Enhanced Defense + Indestructible (If you did not corrupt this first), +1 To All Skills 5% To All Attributes Indestructible if you did and it stacks? Need Confirm, 10% Increased Maximum Life otherwise
  • Shield: 15% Increased Chance of Blocking + 15% Faster Block Rate + Indestructible (If you did not corrupt this first), 10% Increased Chance of Blocking + 15% All Resistance if you did
  • Gloves: Make Eth First, +1 Barbarian Skill Levels, Indestructible, You can also opt for Deadly Strike % here instead based on your solve, it is likely better till you have 2 Jackal's Laughter On
  • Amulet: 50% Enhanced Weapon Damage, -5% to Enemy Physical Resistance
  • Rings: 15% Enhanced Weapon Damage, -3% to Enemy Physical Damage Resistance
  • Belt: +1 Barbarian Skill Levels +5% PDR
  • Boots: +1 Barbarian Skill Levels +5% PDR
  • Helm: Make Eth First, +2 Barbarian Skill Levels + Indestructible (If you did not Corrupt this first), 30 Strength + 30 Vitality or 5% PDR, ITD is ok here as well for white mobs if you want to ignore Inner Sight
