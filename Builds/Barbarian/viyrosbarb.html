  1. INTRO

Hello! My name is Viyro, my background comes from countless hours of ARPG's, Stream Management, and Event Creation. You can find some of my past work in POE via Zizarans Youtube Channel, defining meta builds across many games etc, as well as the Co-Creation of Path of Exiles Gauntlet. Now that thats out the way lets get in to the build.

The purpose of this build is to min-max our ability to do all of the games content including Labyrinth. My latest Diablo Lab kill was under 10 seconds.

My philosophy on Diablo 2 build creation is mostly problem solving first which should hopefully give you insight on gear choices.

To “Solve” a build we must solve all issues that plague a character in Diablo 2. Lab is the ultimate check in to this for your character. In no particular order. We can use a combination of the character and Mercenary to achieve these things. 

Required:

  • Physical immunity breaking
  • Leech
  • 95% Max Res
  • Overcap res to accommodate for Lower Resist and Conviction
  • Physical Damage Reduction to accommodate for Amp / Decrep etc
  • Hit Chance
  • Defense Rating
  • - Enemy Physical Resistance
  • “Crit” - Deadly Strike, Critical Strike Chance
  • Damage
