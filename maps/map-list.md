# Maps and Wardens

Every theme is available in **Tiers 1–6**. The theme determines the setting, monster population, and Warden; the tier and rolled modifiers determine how demanding that particular run will be.

## Map themes

The monsters below are examples of each theme's population. Your map's modifiers can change some of the packs you meet.

| Map | What to expect | Warden dangers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Sandswept Tomb** | Winding desert tunnels leading to a tomb arena. Vipers, greater mummies, maggots, and scarabs. | Holy Shock, Dust Devils, Chain Lightning, and cold melee attacks. |
| **Corrupted Durance** | A cramped Kurast dungeon populated by Council members, vampires, hulking brutes, and fetishes. | Holy Fire, firewalls, Lower Resist, and Council escorts. |
| **Forsaken Catacombs** | Catacomb corridors filled with undead, skeletal archers, vampires, and spiders. | Poison attacks and Plague Pulse, supported by mummy and archer escorts. |
| **Frozen Depths** | Ice caves with frozen horrors, succubi, yetis, and other cold-region enemies. | Holy Freeze, Frozen Orb, Frost Nova, and cold melee attacks. |
| **Worldstone Keep** | Keep halls with Hell Bovines, souls, frenzying melee enemies, and spellcasters. | Fanaticism, a stunning stomp, Baal Nova, and bovine and soul escorts. |
| **Sunscar Dunes** | Open desert with scarabs, leapers, vultures, and ranged hunters. | Holy Shock, Dust Devils, Charged Bolts, and lightning melee attacks. |
| **Forsaken Highlands** | Open highlands with goatmen, corrupted rogues, Fallen, and shamans. | Might, a heavy stomp, stunning attacks, and ranged escorts. |
| **Fallen Travincal** | Ruined temple grounds with Council members, zealots, cantors, and jungle creatures. | Fanaticism, firewalls, Lower Resist, and fire melee attacks. |
| **Ashen Steppes** | Scorched open terrain with large demons, knights, and floating spellcasters. | Holy Fire, Fire Wall, Terror, and fire melee attacks. |
| **Infernal Rift** | Lava passages populated by siege minions, succubi, overseers, and demons. | Might, Meteor, Baal Nova, and fire melee attacks. |

## Fighting a Warden

Explore the main map to find the route into its Warden arena. The Warden is a tougher, themed boss with its own abilities and accompanying monsters. Higher tiers strengthen the fight and increase the escort size.

Prepare for both the Warden's abilities and the map's rolled modifiers. A resistance penalty can make an elemental boss much more dangerous, while a monster aura can make its escorts a serious threat. Give yourself room to move and avoid being surrounded when you enter the arena.

The Warden's reward includes **one map and one mapping currency**, plus regular loot rolls. The rewarded map matches the run's tier, except that a Tier 6 Warden rewards a **Tier 5 map**. The reward's theme can vary.

## Choosing a map for your build

Consider how your build handles narrow passages, open terrain, ranged enemies, and immunities. A layout that suits one character may be awkward for another even at the same tier.

The map's name alone does not tell you its difficulty. Read the [modifiers](/en/maps/modifiers) before committing, and use an [Arcane Relic](/en/maps/crafting) if you want to try for a different roll.
