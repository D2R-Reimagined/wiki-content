# Map Modifiers and Rewards

A map's modifiers tell you how that run differs from another map of the same theme and tier. Check its tooltip before opening it, then use the in-map display to keep track of the active effects.

[Getting started](/en/maps/overview) · [Crafting](/en/maps/crafting) · [Maps and Wardens](/en/maps/map-list) · [Encounters](/en/maps/encounters)

## More monsters and elites

| Modifier | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Teeming** | More monsters. |
| **Swarming** | A larger increase in monster density, with additional elite packs. |
| **Storied** | More elite packs. |
| **Legendary** | A larger increase in elite packs, with extra monster density. |

More enemies mean more opportunities for loot, but also more pressure on your defenses. Many combat modifiers also increase density and elite numbers; check the map's overall bonuses as well as its individual modifier names.

## Player penalties

| Modifier | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Sapping** | −15 percentage points to physical attack damage bonuses. |
| **Withering** | −30 percentage points to physical attack damage bonuses. |
| **Unhallowed** | −2 to all skills. |
| **of Frailty** | −30 to fire, cold, lightning, and poison resistances. |
| **of Ruin** | −60 to fire, cold, lightning, and poison resistances. |
| **of Agony** | −25 to physical resistance. |

Sapping and Withering reduce physical attack damage bonuses; they do not mean that every source of damage you deal is multiplied by 0.85 or 0.70. Spell damage is not their target.

These are temporary map effects, not permanent changes to your character. Follow the scope shown in the tooltip: **map-wide** penalties apply throughout the main area and Warden arena; a tooltip that says **near monsters** limits the effect to that proximity.

Extra resistance above your displayed cap can help absorb Frailty or Ruin. Agony affects physical resistance separately from the four elemental resistances.

## Monster auras

| Modifier | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **of Conviction** | Monsters carry level 10 Conviction, threatening your elemental resistances and defense. |
| **of Might** | Monsters carry level 12 Might, increasing physical attack damage. |
| **of Fanaticism** | Monsters carry level 10 Fanaticism, making attack-based packs more dangerous. |

These auras are in addition to the theme's own Warden abilities. Give aura-supported packs space, especially when several enemies can attack you at once.

## Population and attack modifiers

| Modifier | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Bovine Incursion** | Some packs are replaced by Hell Bovines. |
| **Witch Coven** | Some packs are replaced by Succubi. |
| **Restless Legion** | Some packs are replaced by skeletal archers. |
| **Blood Frenzy** | Ordinary map monsters gain 15% movement and attack speed. |
| **Flame Pact** | Ordinary map monsters add 20–40 fire attack damage per map tier. |
| **Frost Pact** | Ordinary map monsters add 15–30 cold attack damage per map tier. |
| **Storm Pact** | Ordinary map monsters add 5–60 lightning attack damage per map tier. |

Population modifiers change part of the map's monster mix. Blood Frenzy and the elemental Pacts also affect ordinary Warden escorts; they do not replace the Warden's own abilities.

For example, Flame Pact adds 60–120 fire attack damage in Tier 3. It is additional elemental damage on monster attacks, so prepare the appropriate resistance.

## Reward modifiers

| Modifier | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Gilded** | Elites gain a bonus drop chance for gold, gems, Horadric Orbs, or Arcane Relics. |
| **Artificer's** | Elites gain a bonus drop chance for gems, jewels, or equipment. |

These are extra chances on elites, not a guaranteed extra item from every kill. They do not multiply the Warden's rewards or increase treasure-monster encounter frequency.

## Combat Magic Find

Maps provide a **combat Magic Find bonus** while you fight nearby map monsters. It grows with tier and the strength of the rolled modifiers, up to **+250% from the map**. Read the tooltip or in-map display for your run's bonus.

This helps with item quality through normal Magic Find rules. It is not a permanent character bonus or a percentage increase to the number of items dropped. Higher tiers also improve ordinary loot-drop chances, while increased density and elite numbers provide more enemies to loot.

## Choosing a roll

Higher tiers roll more modifiers: **1 / 1–2 / 2 / 2–3 / 3 / 4** from Tier 1 through Tier 6. Some modifiers only become available at higher tiers, and not every combination can appear.

Choose the difficulty your build can clear reliably. If the combination looks dangerous, use **one map + one Arcane Relic** in the Cube on Hell to reroll it before opening. See [Crafting and Progression](/en/maps/crafting) for the full recipes.
