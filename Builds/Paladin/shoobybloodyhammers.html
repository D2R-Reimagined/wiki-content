1. INTRO

The Hammerdin is a retail-favorite build due to how it lines up with that game’s version mechanics, being able to do all content while being very defensive.

Here those things still hold true, but there are a lot of more viable options and greater powerlevel spread towards all builds, making so that the Hammerdin drawbacks become more apparent and the build is a lot less played here – also the fact that people have seen the spinny things go off for 20 years already – but if you eventually end up circling back (hehe) to this class after some good time of Reimagined, there are still some interesting new stuff to try out for the class.

And one of those things is putting the new Prayer synergy to use and abusing Blood Golems for their increased magic damage and Blood Casting effects.

3. REASONS TO PLAY THIS BLESSED HAMMER PALADIN

  1. Damage: damage output on this build actually scales very well (maybe a bit too well) towards the endgame, albeit it being very slow and steady on that front. By the end you are dealing very high single target numbers but, on an area, which makes deleting champion packs in particular very satisfying.
  2. Very defensible, even with gear pushing a little bit too much to the ‘cutesey’ side like the one on this guide (it will make more sense as you read). Paladins are innately good due to how easy it is to get block cap with Holy Shield, and this build has a very food Prayer dimension to it that increases sustain and also some meaty summons.
  3. Cool layers of synergy and fun gearing, specially now with -enemy magic res being an accessible stat and freeing some build requirements (in your merc in particular).

 

4. REASONS TO NOT PLAY THIS BLESSED HAMMER PALADIN

  1. Spell is CLUNKY AS FUCK. Theres not much dancing around this one. Anyone that played hammers know what it about. The spell has its own particular pattern, it hits stuff that blocks it path all the time, and its not particularly fast. It’s the kind of thing that you play if you like or if you ‘had’ to subject to it due to lack of options like it is on Retail (where FoH is only a real option for a very specific targeted farming reason, but not for actual wide play). Attempts where made in the last few patches to better Hammerdin play, in particular on thigh corridor areas with the addition of Holy Bolt on the build tree, but that only helps a tiny bit.
  2. Very slow density clear compared to the more flashy newer causing Fist of Heavens paladin. Both build kinda play in the same niche (caster Paladin magic damage dealer), but FoH line up a lot more favorably against the more usual content current in game. Hammerdin has its benefits (mostly in the boss damage front), but if that is good enough to pick it over FoH, it’s a question for you alone to answer. Good thing is that the transition between both builds is pretty easy, as you use a lot of overlapping gear, so no reason you can’t have the best of both worlds.

 

5. MAIN STATS

Regular decades-old D2 gearing heuristic. Put whatever you need in STR to wear gear. Put how much you feel you need (or can spare, if needed) in DEX to achieve max block (or any point you feel comfortable with). Everything else into VIT, the most you can.

Usually I don’t mention other stats breakpoints to aim for on my guides, because whatever you get with the main gear suggestions I made will land you in a good spot, but here I’ll call out the need to be cast rate capped (125%FCR) because otherwise you will feel super sluggish without it and there are some random FCR range gear that you will have to juggle around and keep in mind as you gear.

 

6. ENDGAME SKILL BUILD

For leveling, you can pick whatever flavor of Paladin you prefer (the good old reliable Holy Fire Paladin still as good as always), but if you are a madman and decides to level as a Hammer guy to enjoy the game journey ‘as you would’ like I do usually, you will have to rely a lot into Holy Bolt for a good portion of the game, and find generalist good caster gear and ways to sustain your mana.

Now, for the endgame build at lvl 100 with all extra skill points:

  • 25 Prayer (Synergy and boosts your self healing to increase the sustain of your merc and summons, support Blood Casting and Life & Death charm)
  • 1 Might (Pre-req)
  • 1 Blessed Aim (Pre-req)
  • 25 Concentration (Blessed Hammer ‘pseudo-synergy’ and a very good boost to yours and mercenary damage).
  • 25 Holy Bolt (Synergy, ‘thigh space tool’, and something to use when you have to be at range for some reason. Weaker than FoH version is due to not having 1 extra synergy ‘on the way back’, but it is still does decent enough damage to help in places Hammer is just impossible to use properly).
  • 25 Blessed Hammer (The name of the build, main damage tool).
  • Everything else you can put into various utility auras as you like, or into Defiance for Holy Shield synergy.

Talking about Holy Shield, in the endgame setup you don’t have to put points into it. Your weapon will grant you points into it, so you don’t have to spend 3 points to get it. But during regular gameplay, ofc invest what you feel you need to get it and have decent block % increase, or try to get a +Holy Shield Large Charm with it.

 

7. GEAR SETUP 

Gearing for this build is very particular in that I’m trying to stuff a bunch of stuff that is made viable for the build by using some low and mid level gear that absolutely lack in some departments when compared to a more usual straightforward high level gear setup.

What it ends up resulting is a mix of gear that particularly lacks some innate values on %PDR (very dangerous on a caster build that will have to put itself in the middle of packs of enemies to kill them) and resists.

This means that you will have to, invariable, dedicate a very specific effort in corrupting and enchanting gear to fill out those gaps.

As usual, for most gear with these kind of chars +1 all skill corrupts are the theoretical best corruptions for gears, but because of how rare it is and how demanding finding better sources of PDR% (and less, elemental resists, but still needed) I found myself having to farm multiple copies of gearpieces aiming for the 5%PDR/5%Magic Resist or the 10% all resists/2% max resists corruptions on every piece of gear that could roll it.

Keep that in mind when gearing up, and fill the gaps you have with some of the alternative suggestions I’ll list while you need, or with things like resists charms.

 

7.1. HEAD SLOT - OVERLORD'S HELMET

I’ll start saying that I don’t particularly like this item, but it felt like a needed sacrifice because of the need of FCR. Ther 20-25% FCR coupled with 3 all skills at the top is very very good, but I intended to use some good rolled crafted paladin Diadems I have laying around. Those are the theoretical BiS for this slot, but I didn’t got lucky to have some rolled with FCR numbers to support and since I had to sacrifice a Ring slot to have some %PDR (more on that below) and enchant slots for more life and LPK numbers, this was the slot I found to fill that gap.

It is a good output piece anyway, and you wont feel inadequate about using it. Just with it had some more defensive numbers to it.

Enchant it with 3x +2 Magic Skills. All Skills is good too, particularly at the start, but by the end the extra damage is better as you reach enough +all skills with the rest of the gear.

For corruptions, either +all skills or 5/5 PDR/MR or 10/2 Res/Max Res.

7.1.1. ALTERNATIVE OPTIONS FOR HELM

Besides a good 2/2 all/paladin skills crafted Diadem cited above, something like Royal Diadem is actually not bad since the fact it gives a lot of attribute points and Salavation Aura (that frees on gear slot on your merc) would be a very good piece to lighten the gear requirements, if you can afford to slot it in.

A good option for leveling and early gearing is Warlock’s Exploration set diadem Lilarcor’s Crown. It gives 2 all skills and good resists, and enables other set bonuses for other pieces if you use them.

Finally, while you don’t have enough Heaven Facets to jewel your Overlords, using a Knight’s Vigil is honestly the best 1 slot option you could have here – due to how much FCR it give and also because it had Meditation aura – which also makes so you can use a better shield before having to swap to the recommended one on this guide. 

 

7.2. GLOVE SLOT – BLINDED BY FAITH

Another HEAVILY damage-skewed gear option, but this one I don’t feel like trading. It has everything you want: FCR, up to 2 magic skills (don’t accept less than that), -enemy magic res, %magic skill damage. It comes at the cost of having very variable ranges on everything you want, making farming for a good corrupted copy an ordeal, and also reducing your own magic resist for a significant number; but they are very very good.

For upgrades, I ended up doing Life + Life per Kill enchants here to enhance the character sustain.

For corruptions, you can get some FCR here if you are lacking in other places, or the very best one would be Iron Skin (another 1% corrupt).

7.2.1. ALTERNATIVE OPTIONS FOR GLOVES

Lachdanan’s is a … decent… option. It gives up to 2 skills, even though you cant put the physical leaning attributes to use.

Another, better option is also the Warlock’s Exploration set gloves Teleomortis’ Gloves. They only give 10% magic skill damage by themselves, but if you are using them in conjunction with at least one more of that set items, it also gives 15% FCR. And if you are using it with 3 items (which is very defensible thing to do in game progression as you usually have a lot more spare Assemblage Orbs available to roll on gear at this point), it also gives -10% to enemy magic resist. It’s a very good combination of items to use in early gear levels to farm Hell Terrorzones too, since you may take a good while to farm a good pair of Blinded by Faith.

 

7.3. ARMOR SLOT – GOLEM'S GAIN

Yup, these is the first place where weird gearing comes at a price but not the last one.

This armor piece grants you with some STR, that will help you to equip your Blinded by Faith, and it gives you both Golem Mastery and Blood Golem (and Clay Golem charges too, but that’s not much of a biggie).

During very early game, this will carry you through the game due to the fact of maging life is actually less of a bore than managing mana, and the Golems soak a lot of damage and don’t die as the game is very easy by this point. On mid to early post-game progression, I wouldn’t use this item as keeping Blood Golem alive will be a chore. But, on late gear stages keeping them alive (you will be able to use 2 golems no problem) becomes barely an issue again, and the damage boost this piece provides is bigger than any other piece of gear would grant you.

The cost though is that besides that, the piece doesn’t provide you much. Its defense is low (specially since uptiering it to an Elite version screw up your gearing a lot due to the high STR requirement it will have), and you lose the opportunity cost of using a good rolled 2/2 all/paladin craft roll with a lot of PDR% and resist numbers, and maybe even an additional good defensive aura like Meditation or Cleansing. But hey, those are the sacrifices we make when we want to roll a fun character xD.

For enchants, I ended up doing 5x 5 all resist 1 max all resist enchants here, but 5x 8%PDR enchants are also very good. You will have to judge which one completes your gear necessity better.

For corruptions, either +all skills or 5/5 PDR/MR or 10/2 Res/Max Res.

7.3.1. ALTERNATIVE OPTIONS FOR CHEST ARMOR

If not going for Blood Golems aspect of this build, then Legacy of the Eternals is a very good balanced gear piece defensive and offensive wise. It gives a lot of max resists, and 2 all skills.

Other option in the unique/set range is Celestial Unicorn. This armor gives you 2 all skills and Cleansing aura, which gives you an additional Prayer Ping (more on it below) and frees up your boot slot to use some more defensive piece than the one that will be recommended below.

The theoretical BiS though would be a crafted piece with high PDR and All resist numbers, 2 extra paladin skill levels and either Meditation or Cleansing aura.

 

7.4. BELT SLOT – GHEED'S WAGER

If you thought farming for a good pair of corrupted Blinded by Faith was painful, WELCOME TO HELL.

This piece is pretty good, but it can drive you mad. It gives 1 all skills (or not), good resists (or the absolute opposite of that and makes you punch a wall), FCR and -enemy magic resist.

And when you can get a good version of it, rest assured you will brick your corruption in the most catastrophic ways possible haha. Still, it’s a worthy grind if you want a good character.

For enchants, at the end I was able to slap 3x flat life and mana on it, but 3x 6 all attributes and resists is what I would recommend on this slot. It would lighten the gearing requirements needs and help out with your defensive stats.

For corruptions, either +all skills or 5/5 PDR/MR or 10/2 Res/Max Res.

7.4.1. ALTERNATIVE OPTIONS FOR BELT

The best early alternative for this slot is definitely once again Warlock’s Exploration piece in Citadel Belt. It doesn’t have many stats besides 2 all skills (quite a bit on a belt slot), but it’s a good slot to enable the 2 and 3 piece bonuses of those sets for Gloves and Headpice, which end up being a very good synergistic little suite of items to use during progression.

Another option is Cortex Wrap (Sine’s Psionics set). It gives a good chunck of elemental absorbs, and when used with the recommended boots, it also gives 1 all skills.

Finally, if going for a lot more defensiveness, Ogre's Embrace or Kashya's are still very good for this slot.

 

7.5. BOOT SLOT – PHASEWALKER BOOTS

The one and only reason to use these boots its because since this build is trying to juggle both a double stack of Prayer pings (more on that later) AND Blood Golems all together, this is the slot you can squeeze your Cleansing source on.

(Well, I guess it has Warp to act as a pseudo teleport while you don’t have your full upgrade Anni I guess).

If you happen to forge of one of the builds aspect though, these should be the first item to go, since it really provides you with nothing good if you have Cleansing going elsewhere (or simply doesn’t care about it).

IMAGE

For upgrades, one of the life enhancing enchants should be optimal. I slapped 8% life and mana because why not.

For corruptions, either 5/5 PDR/MR or 10/2 Res/Max Res.

7.5.1. ALTERNATIVE OPTIONS FOR BOOTS

For a balanced approach, Lustwander is a pretty good slot, with 1 all skills, 25 resists and actual Teleport – trumping almost everything you can easily achieve on boots slot thinking of casters output.

Zero Effect would be the better choice when you have Teleport elsewhere and if not using Phasewalker boots. Its defensive range is unmatched on this slot and will make your more durable and free a lot of stat realstate by itself alone under those circumstances.

 

7.6. WEAPON SLOT – CRUSADER'S WRATH

This is your perfect weapon. It grants you potential huge numbers one your most important skills (Hammers, Concentration and Holy Shield so you don’t have to put points into it), HUGE FCR numbers, HUGE -enemy resist numbers, some STR to make equipping Blinded by Faith easier, and some Vitality.

The downside is that it, once again, has a very large field of variance regarding rolls; so farming for a perfect copy something that can be tedious. Regarding that, it has two good benefits though: 1) its on the same base as Hand of the Blessed Light, the best FoH weapon, meaning you can do dual work to farm good copies of both weapons at the same time and 2) it really doesn’t need a Corruption on it (the only benefit is +1 all skills, a 1% chance corruption), so you realistically don’t have to risk losing a good copy.

For upgrades, 5x 2/2 +magic skill damage -enemy magic res enchants are a lock.

7.6.1. ALTERNATIVE OPTIONS FOR WEAPONS

For leveling you will most likely use a Spirit for quite a few levels. Other than that, really no use in thinking about any other piece on this slot.

 

7.7. SHIELD SLOT - CRUSADER'S WILL

Mid-level caster shield that is slotted in because of Meditation aura, which makes it have its value on this build. It wont be too much behind the best shield for this slot in terms of damage, but it does lag considerably behind regarding block chance, resists and just overall ‘chonkyness’ you would want in a shield for endgame. 

For enchants, 5x 3 all res 1 max res.

For corruptions, either 5/5 PDR/MR or 10/2 Res/Max Res.

7.7.1. ALTERNATIVE OPTIONS FOR SHIELD SLOT

In a balanced approach, God’s Word would be the de-facto best shield for a Hammerdin if you are not trying to slot so many mechanics into a single character. Even if it does seem more tailor made for FoH, the shiled has very good numbers and balance of skills, resists, armor, FCR and very good -enemy resist numbers.

For a very defensive piece, Fortress of Morpheus would be perfect on this slot.

Both of these options are very good to use while you don’t have enough Heavens Facet to shove on all of your gear, making so the best helm you can use is a Knight’s Vigil intead of Overlord’s, meaning you get Meditation from that and are free to use this better shields. If you don’t care about the Prayer mechanic going on the background of the build (more on it later), pick one of these 2. 

 

7.8. JEWELRY SLOTS

The range of jewelry, specially regarding rare and crafted options is absurdly huge on this mod.

Even keeping to only Uniques and Set options, the variance in what you can do on these slots is huge and it comes a lot down to what you decide to use on the other gear slots.

The best damage-oriented option is for sure Sling, the new ROTW addition. The ring is SUPER good. It has variable FCR and -enemy magic resistance numbers and 1 to magic skills.

In theory using 2 of these along with a neckpiece like Maras/Hope Sigil would be the best to do when you are not spreading your gearing thin like I am on this particular build, but I felt I could only afford 1 of them and have the other ring (as long as with the neckpiece) be a Lords of Hell set 2-piece. With these 2 items you get 5 all skills (which is a very very good number on an amulet+ring combo if don’t consider nasty-rolled rares and crafted items, which can pretty much happen on this mod), and the main reason I decided to use the combo was that along with 10% PDR the ring gives, which I very much needed to try to get closer to 50% PDR on this char.

Enchant for rings I ended up doing 2x PDR% on these, even if they are only worth 4% a piece on these slots. But like mentioned, I REALLY needed some extra %PDR.

For amulet, 2x Paladin skills is a lock.

For corruptions, either +all skills or 5/5 PDR/MR or 10/2 Res/Max Res.

7.8.1. ALTERNATIVE OPTIONS FOR JEWELRY

There is a vast array of option here, but some noteworthy stuff to mention in case you wanna look for alternatives:

Amulets: Sigil of Hope, Mara’s Kaleidoscope, Foul Sigil, Luck Sigil. METALGRID is also something I theorized a lot and with I could afford it on the build. Maybe I can if I get lucky with some other gear pieces.

Rings: Fellowship's Hope would be a more defensive-leaning allstar to use on this slot. Faerie Rings are pretty good too. One Nature’s Piece could be an superb HP Pool increase for you and your mec and summons if your gearing allows it. Bunch of options.

 

7.9. WEAPON SWAP SLOT

On alternate slot, for this build the uncontested swap is Warrior Untamed and a 2 all skill base shield (something like Valorsong or uptiered Wisdom of Toth) all filled with Heaven facets is the most straightforward option.

Another thing you can use though, since you are a paladin, is using a Voice of the Prophet for lvl 30 charges of all the 3 beneficial shouts (Shout, BO and BC), and then use something like a rare one-hander with Fade charges (or something like an Invisible Stalker) for even more benefits.

This approach though is quite a bit of maintanace as Voice of the Prophet only has 10 charges and is very expensive to repair, and also means your shoult levels will never be more than 30 (you can reach 40+ pretty easy with all skill gearing this build does, and this is less of an issue as the most important shout – BO – can be accessed at lvl 40 anyways due to your merc Colossal Jewel), but if you can find a good source of Fade charges, it theoretically can be very good as Fade will make things easier on the resist and %PDR fronts, helping to use better gear elsewhere.

I’m still on the hunt of a good one-hand source of Fade to enable this for myself.

7.11. CHARMS

A +2 Queen’s Call and +2 Life & Death are particularly good, alongside Annihilus and Obsidian Beacon. You can use the Small Charms with Life and Mana too for some more numbers there, but at some point you will want more Skillers and good LCs.

Those, by the way, can roll some very decent combinations on Large Charms, as the Paladin affixes for those are STR and VIT with up to +3 of any of those skills, and if you are absurdly lucky you can even roll another useful Suffix like FCR or -enemy magic res with it. I never had the luck to get one of these in any char I actually play, but they are possible.

If you get enough surplus resistances that you don’t mind using the retail version of the renewed sunder charms, they can provide a lot of damage with their % damage and -res stats.

 

8. MERCENARY

Here you have a lot of options, but for me there are 2 clear main contenteders:

The more usual one for these sort of builds, is an A5 Frenzy merc that stacks Sanctuary aura and Weaken procs. This are the clear cut better option for me at the start of the post-hell gearing process, as by that point you will put all the benefits of Sanctuary source to use (as you are locked into using Concentration yourself and your -magic resist numbers aren’t there by yourself). This is a very good merc too because it is more easily translated into the better FoH mercenary, if you like to swap between those 2 specs, as he can both do the aforementioned Sanctuary if you don’t like to use Sanctuary yourself as a FoH pally (something I don’t consider optimal but some people do it) or it can be the best Weaken proccer with dual Heron-branded Blades (FoH pallies tend to have a little bit less innate -enemy magic res as their gear have more %magic damage due to considerations as they can use Sanctuary aura). This is also the chonkiest merc by itself, and it can really be a boss melting machine by itself if you stack a lot of CB on it, which you easily can. In almost all scenarios, for me, this is are the best builds to put these mercs to use.

For pure Hammers though, on this build in particular, I think that by the end using an A2 Defiance merc is defensible as the best tailored as you can make do with lower Sanctuary and Weaken procs, and its innate aura frees up a gear slot indirectly:

Since you are using Blood golems and you don’t have access to Summon Resist, you absolute need a Salvation source to have them be more resilient, and since they are constantly in the middle of melee and getting attacked, you also can put Defiance aura to good use (also as your own defensive numbers are not that great, you yourself can put that to good use). This mean there is some strain on the A5 merc trying to accomplish both Defiance and Salvation aura as you will want your weapon slots tied up to Sanctuary/Weaken weapons if you are going to use them (otherwise, why would you). 

The best I could make a setup for an A5 merc taking those into account granted a LVL 1 Salvation and LVL 18 Defiance, and no extra benefits. For an A2 I wa able to get LVL 26+ Defiance, LVL 5 Salvation and still have the headpiece slot free for Blindsight, which indirectly increases safety A LOT for your party due to it blinding entire packs of mobs, making them less aggressive and attacking you a lot less.

  • Belt: Kashya's Ward.
  • Boots: Zero Effect for a lot of defensive stats, or Hallowed Grounds for more Sanctuary levels.
  • Rings: 1x Bul-Katho's or Jackal’s for skills and life leech, along with physical damage stats. Or Chiad’s for Holy Freeze aura.
  • Amulet: Vanquisher for a bunch of damage, or some good all around neck with stats, skills, resists or whatever. Sigil of Hope feels like a good all arounder here.

A2 Defiance Merc:

  • Weapon: Spirit Light. This one gives your merc up to lvl 15 Sancturay (-25 enemy magic res), which compliments what you already have pretty well as it doesnt ‘cost’ anything to have. Besides CBF, it doesn’t give you much innately, but it gives LVL 5 Salvation for your entire party, which is super needed in the build.
  • Gloves: Souldrainer. It gives lvl 5 Weaken on Strike. 9. GAMEPLAY LOOP

Regular gameplay loop consists of:

  • Buff up with Battle Command.
  • Check which set gives you the better golems.
  • Summon them in the respective best sets.
  • Rebuff BC and Buff Battle Orders and Shout to buff you and all your fwends.
  • Swap to main setup.
  • Buff Buff Holy Shield.
  • Go out and mash stuff

 

10. MECHANICS

10.1. CONCENTRATION AURA

Concentration aura is your aura of ‘choice’, without it there is no Hammer Damage. The aura effect is 60% of damage at lvl 1 + 15% damage per aura level.

Its benefit to Hammers though is half of that, but still is the reason the spell’s damage gets boosted to absurd levels. It will be the case that by endgame you will have over 1200% tooltip damage bonus, which will make your hammers deal 6x and probably a lot more times than its tooltip damage.

10.2. PRAYER HEALING

Prayer is an aura that provides health recovery in fixed 2 second ticks in the mod. The most important thing is that Prayer also grants equal benefit of its effect to both Meditation and Cleansing auras, and these all stack up, even when the aura itself is not active. This mean that if you get these 2 auras running on your gear, you can get passive heal going while still wielding your Concentration aura.

Achieving this has real costs on the itemization (as it can be seen by all the discussion on the gearing chapter), but it can be done in a few ways, and in my opinion even if you just use it in a not heavy handed manner as it is on this guide, it has its benefits. For example, you will max Prayer anyway, and with decent gear you will have it hit healing values in the 100s, so even if you gear as a regular no-nonsense Hammer Paladin would (aka, no Blood Golem nonses, trying to use only the higher level best items), there is very little cost into slotting a Celestial Unicorn in the armor slot and getting the benefit of a single Prayer healing benefit on there. This already gives you some good benefit, and its specially “cost-less” if you can achieve that by using some godly rolled crafted armors that come with either Meditation or Cleansing on its rolls.

And this is also true for FoH builds. Spending a single point on (or using a Large Charm with) Prayer already will give you access to a very considerable Prayer thick due to all the +all skills that build also uses, and with a single Meditation or Cleansing item you also already reap the benefits. I’ll give and example of this down below.

10.3. BLOOD GOLEM

Blood golem is your most important summon. It provides 2 layers of benefits you need to have to make the build functional.

First one is Bloodcasting: The golem provides you with an aura that makes your skills cost health instead of mana. While that can (and should imo) be abused by any magic caster build. The golem itself provides you with a way to offset this cost, by also having a Life per Kill aura. Most of the time wont be enough to offset the costs entirely, so you will need extra sources of health regen and LPK while you level. At the end though, with your golem supercharged by all the +ask items you have and since you are also dishing out big ticks of Prayer healing, you shouldn’t need much besides of what the build passively gives you.

Second benefit is the golem aura also increases magic spell damage. On versions past the aura gave a different effect that would limit its potential to the specific necro spells, but that was changed since 3.0. It was made to be something balanced for Bone Necros, and it honestly feels a little bit of an abuse on other builds, but at least it comes at a tangible cost on the paladin builds (on Abyss warlock on the other hand, its just dumb since you can get the benefit out of weapons, and 2-handed weapons at that, which means double magic damage enchants too. Nothing new considering this god forsaken class just breaks everything in this game).

 

11. FOH SWAP

So you prefer using FoH but still wanna keep the synergy layers presented in this guide? That’s completely possible to some extent.

Just swap your weapon from a Crusader’s Wrath to a Hand of the Blessed light, and respect accordingly:

  • 25 pointers: Holy Bolt, Blessed Hammer, Fist of the Heavens, Sanctuary.
  • 1 pointers: Holy Shield and PRAYER (you have to have this one pointer if not accessing it via a LC)

Since you will be self wielding Sanctuary, equip your mercenary to take advantage of that, like Swapping your Azurewraths for uptiered Heron-branded Blades on a A5 Frenzy merc for example.

And that’s basically it. Very straightforward actually. Your Prayer ticks will be a bit lower, but still will be very strong and you will be more safe most of the time since FoH is a very long rage and safe spell to use, specially with some meat shields running interception for you.
