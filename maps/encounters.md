# Map Events and Treasure Monsters

Explore the main map for optional encounters as well as the route to its Warden. These encounters provide additional combat and rewards without replacing the Warden fight.

[Getting started](/en/maps/overview) · [Crafting](/en/maps/crafting) · [Maps and Wardens](/en/maps/map-list) · [Modifiers](/en/maps/modifiers)

## Map events

At the default settings, a map has a **30% chance to select an event**, with at most one of the following three events per run. Watch for an event banner and direction arrow as you get close; they are proximity guides, so an empty display at the entrance does not mean the map has no event.

### Treasure Raiders

A **Treasure Raider** appears with a group of guards. Defeat the treasure bearer for bonus loot and crafting rewards. The bearer carries the event reward; the guards are there to protect it.

There is no chase timer. Approach the group on your terms and avoid getting surrounded.

### Dark Challenger

Find and activate a **Dark Sigil** to summon a Challenger suited to the map's theme and tier, with accompanying enemies. Be ready to fight before clicking it.

Defeating the Challenger awards loot and mapping currency. This is a separate encounter from the Warden, so you can complete both in the same run.

### Sealed Reliquary

Activate a **Sealed Reliquary** to begin three waves:

1. Four Reliquary Guardians.
2. Six Reliquary Guardians.
3. A Reliquary Keeper.

Defeat each wave to progress. There is **no time limit**, and the **Keeper carries the completion reward**. Do not leave after the early waves expecting the full payout.

The reward can focus on crafting materials, equipment, or maps and mapping currency. The reward type is random. If the encounter cannot place the next enemies, clear space around the cache and activate it again.

## Rare treasure monsters

Treasure monsters are separate from map events. At the default settings, a map has an **8% combined chance** to select one of these encounters, equally split between the two types. A map can contain both a treasure monster and a map event.

| Monster | Reward |
| --- | --- |
| **Jewel Hoarder** | Six jewels, with normal jewel quality rolls. |
| **Amulet Collector** | Six amulets, with rare quality guaranteed unless a normal set or unique roll succeeds first. |

There is at most **one treasure monster per map**. These are bonus enemies in the main area, separate from the Warden and its escorts. Look for their names and discovery marker while exploring.

Higher tiers make them stronger and raise item levels; they do not increase the six-item reward count. More density, more elites, Magic Find, and reward modifiers do not increase their appearance chance. Magic Find can still matter to normal item-quality rolls.

## Returning to an encounter

Leaving the map for town and returning preserves the same encounter and its progress. It does not select a new event or respawn a defeated treasure monster. Start a new game for a fresh run of the same theme and tier.

The stated chances are the defaults, not a guarantee that an encounter will appear every few maps. Explore thoroughly, and treat these rewards as a bonus to the regular map and Warden loot.
